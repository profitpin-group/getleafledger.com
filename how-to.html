How do I download LeafLedger for Windows?

  1. Recommended: get LeafLedger from the Microsoft Store. Microsoft checks and signs Store apps, so Windows installs it without an unknown-publisher warning.
  2. Or download the installer. If Windows shows a "protected your PC" notice, choose More info → Run anyway.
  3. Open LeafLedger from the Start menu (or the desktop icon if you used the installer). It opens in your web browser. The page runs on your own computer.
  4. The first time, read the one-time agreement, tick the box and click I agree.

To close the app, click Quit LeafLedger at the top of the page.

How do I set up my tobacco business in LeafLedger?

You do this once. Open Setup. Describe the business exactly as TTB and FDA know it.

  1. Companies: enter a short Id and the Legal name. Click Add company.
  2. Permits and reporting scopes: pick the company, the Role (Manufacturer (TTB 5210.5) or Importer (TTB 5220.6)) and the Permit number. Click Add permit. Then add each product class the permit reports and click Add scope. A permit with no scope is flagged as required.
  3. Products: enter an Id (for example your SKU), Name, Class, Origin (domestic or imported) and Base unit. Click Add product. To add another unit, type it in the Units column, for example thousand_sticks=1000.
  4. Source file layouts: click Add profile with standard columns, then Edit columns if your file uses other headers. See the next section.
  5. Account mapping: map each value in your Account column to one LeafLedger transaction type and click Map. If your file already uses LeafLedger's names, click Use standard names.
  6. Evidence documents: add copies such as TTB, CBP or FDA forms and operations records for a month. Click Add evidence.
  7. Company identity for the forms: EIN, Contact name, Telephone, Email and Mailing address.
  8. Mapping approval: the person responsible enters their name under Approved by and clicks Use now.
  9. Click Save setup. When nothing is missing you see Saved — setup complete. Otherwise the page lists what to complete.

How do I import monthly tobacco records from CSV or Excel?

LeafLedger reads UTF-8 CSV files and Excel .xlsx files. Older .xls files are not read; save them as .xlsx or CSV first.

Your file needs one column for each of these facts. These are the standard header names (click Download standard CSV template in Setup to get them):

Transaction ID Company Permit Transaction Date SKU Account Quantity Unit Tax Paid Document

Optional standard columns: Entry Number, Reversal Of, Source Form, Source Field, Source Record, Source Period, Adjustment Basis, Adjustment Reason, Customs Regime. If your headers are different, use Edit columns to match them. For an Excel file, the profile's sheet name (default Records) must match the sheet in your file.

  1. Open Monthly cycle.
  2. Type the Reporting month as year-month, for example 2026-09, and click Load month.
  3. Choose the Profile, choose the file, and click Add source. Repeat for each file that month.

How do I reconcile cigarette and tobacco inventory each month?

  1. In Monthly cycle, under Plan, pick last month's result in Previous month's filing (continuity). This lets LeafLedger check this month's opening stock against last month's closing stock. For your first month, leave — first month —.
  2. Click Prepare this month. LeafLedger reads every row, checks every rule and renders the drafts.
  3. Read the result and click Open review.
  4. If you prepare the same month again, enter a Correction reason. Each preparation is saved as a new result; nothing is overwritten.
  5. After the first preparation, you can choose extra items under Selections after the first preparation (for example a filing jurisdiction such as US-CO for deadlines) and click Prepare again with these selections.

Preparing a month needs an active trial or subscription. For the idea behind the check, see the free roll-forward check and reconciling records before monthly reporting.

How to fill out TTB Form 5210.5 with LeafLedger

LeafLedger prepares a 5210.5 draft for each permit set up with the Manufacturer (TTB 5210.5) role.

  1. Set up the manufacturer permit, its product classes and products (Setup).
  2. Add the month's files and click Prepare this month (Monthly cycle).
  3. Open Review & export. Find the card TTB 5210.5 draft. Each line shows the Draft value and Where it comes from. Zero lines are hidden; click Show … zero line(s) to see them.
  4. Check every line against your own records before anyone signs.

Form background: TTB 5210.5 guide · spreadsheet method.

How to prepare the TTB 5220.6 importer report

  1. Set up the permit with the Importer (TTB 5220.6) role, and set imported products to Origin imported.
  2. Add the month's importer file with an importer profile, then click Prepare this month.
  3. In Review & export, read the TTB 5220.6 draft card line by line.
  4. If the review asks for an official copy such as CBP 7501, add it under Evidence documents in Setup for that month and prepare again.

Form background: TTB 5220.6 importer guide.

How to prepare the FDA 3852 tobacco user fee report

  1. Prepare the month as above. The FDA 3852 draft is built from the same records.
  2. In Review & export, find the FDA 3852 draft card and check each line and its source.
  3. Fix anything flagged, then save or print the draft (see below).

How do I read and fix the checks LeafLedger flags?

LeafLedger never guesses a missing fact. It shows it, so you can fix it. Open Review & export, pick the Result and click Load.

  1. Read Status. Numbers shows RECONCILED or NEED ATTENTION. It also shows the month's state, Blocking exceptions and Open items.
  2. Exceptions in the numbers lists each problem with its code and the source row.
  3. Filing readiness gaps has a What to do column. Monthly checks has a What it means column.
  4. Fix the cause in Setup or in your source file, save, and prepare the month again with a correction reason.

Common codes:

  • PREVIOUS_FILING_REQUIRED — pick last month's filing under Plan.
  • MISSING_OFFICIAL_SUPPORT — add the named official copy (for example TTB 5000.24 or CBP 7501) as evidence for the month.
  • OFFICIAL_RENDERING_UNVALIDATED — drafts are watermarked; review the PDF against your own records.
  • An account value with no mapping stops the month. Add it under Account mapping.

Practise this safely in step 6 of the tutorial.

How do I save, print or export my drafts?

At the top of the loaded result in Review & export:

  1. Print / save as PDF prints the on-screen summary.
  2. Export CSV (draft lines) saves every draft line, its value, unit and basis.
  3. Email summary opens your email program with a short summary. Attach the PDFs yourself.
  4. The draft PDFs are listed under Exported files. Click Open output folder to see them on your computer.
  5. When nothing is left unresolved, Reviewed copies shows Mark reviewed and produce clean copies. Until then it explains why the button isn't there yet.

Reviewing and exporting months you already prepared does not need an active subscription.

How do I back up LeafLedger or move it to another computer?

Everything is in one workspace folder on your computer. By default it is a folder named LeafLedger in your Windows user folder. Open workspace folder on the Overview shows it.

Encrypted backup

  1. On Overview, under Encrypted backup, type a passphrase of 12 or more characters.
  2. Click Create backup. The file is written to the workspace's backups folder.
  3. Copy that file somewhere safe. The passphrase is never stored. Without it, the backup cannot be opened.

Move to another computer

  1. Click Quit LeafLedger.
  2. Copy the whole workspace folder to the same place on the new computer.
  3. Install LeafLedger there, accept the agreement, and enter your license key under Subscription.

Your subscription covers computers your business controls, for one permittee business.

Is LeafLedger for tobacco shops, distributors or vape taxes?

Is LeafLedger for a tobacco shop's monthly tax report?

No. LeafLedger is for businesses that hold a TTB permit as a domestic manufacturer of tobacco products or as a tobacco importer. Retail tobacco shops and smoke shops without a TTB reporting obligation are not who it is built for.

Is LeafLedger for a tobacco distributor's monthly report?

No. Distributors and wholesalers with no TTB reporting obligation are not who it is built for.

Does LeafLedger do vape tax reporting or state tobacco tax returns?

No. LeafLedger prepares drafts of federal TTB and FDA forms. It does not prepare state excise tax returns, and vapes are not among the product classes it lists.

Does LeafLedger cover other tobacco products (OTP)?

Yes, for manufacturers and importers. The product classes in the app are small and large cigarettes, small and large cigars, pipe tobacco, roll-your-own tobacco, chewing tobacco, snuff, cigarette papers, cigarette tubes and processed tobacco.

Does LeafLedger file my tobacco tax report?

No. LeafLedger prepares drafts. You review them, and you or your representative sign and submit them.
